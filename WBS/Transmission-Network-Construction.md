# WBS — Transmission Network Construction

Project: Transmission Network Construction — Work Breakdown Structure

Summary
- Detailed WBS for the planning, construction, testing, and commissioning of a high-voltage transmission line and associated works.
- Use the WBS IDs for schedule, cost estimating, responsibility assignment, and risk tracking.

How to use
- Each element has an ID (level-based numbering). Add Owner, Estimated Duration, Start/End Dates, Dependencies, Status, and Cost for each item.

1. Project Management
  1.1 Project initiation
    - 1.1.1 Project charter & scope confirmation
    - 1.1.2 Stakeholder register
    - 1.1.3 Kick-off workshop
  1.2 Project planning
    - 1.2.1 Master schedule
    - 1.2.2 Project execution plan
    - 1.2.3 Procurement plan
    - 1.2.4 HSE plan
    - 1.2.5 Quality management plan
  1.3 Project controls
    - 1.3.1 Cost control & budgeting
    - 1.3.2 Progress reporting
    - 1.3.3 Change control
    - 1.3.4 Risk & issues management
  1.4 Project closeout
    - 1.4.1 Final reporting
    - 1.4.2 Lessons learned
    - 1.4.3 Contract close-out

2. Surveys, Permitting & Land Rights
  2.1 Route reconnaissance
  2.2 Topographic & geotechnical surveys
    - 2.2.1 Soil investigations for foundations
    - 2.2.2 Hydrology & flood risk assessment
  2.3 Environmental & social baseline studies
  2.4 Permitting & approvals
    - 2.4.1 Environmental permits
    - 2.4.2 Construction permits
    - 2.4.3 Wayleave / easement agreements
  2.5 Land acquisition & compensation

3. Route Finalisation & Design
  3.1 Route optimization & corridor selection
  3.2 Preliminary design
    - 3.2.1 Line profile and stringing clearances
    - 3.2.2 Preliminary tower locations and types
    - 3.2.3 Preliminary foundation types
  3.3 Detailed design
    - 3.3.1 Civil/structural designs (foundations, access)
    - 3.3.2 Tower/tower foundation design drawings
    - 3.3.3 Electrical design (conductor selection, insulators, hardware)
    - 3.3.4 Protection & control design for line and substations
    - 3.3.5 SCADA/communication requirements
  3.4 Design review and approvals

4. Procurement & Logistics
  4.1 Procurement management
    - 4.1.1 Tendering and bid evaluation
    - 4.1.2 Contract award
  4.2 Materials & equipment procurement
    - 4.2.1 Towers and fittings
    - 4.2.2 Conductors, shield wires, OPGW
    - 4.2.3 Insulators, clamps, vibration dampers
    - 4.2.4 Foundations materials (reinforcement, concrete)
    - 4.2.5 Stringing equipment, winches, tensioners
    - 4.2.6 Heavy-lift cranes and transport vehicles
  4.3 Vendor management & QA/QC of supplied materials
  4.4 Logistics & stockyards
    - 4.4.1 Transport to stockyard
    - 4.4.2 Site storage and handling

5. Site Preparation & Civil Works
  5.1 Site mobilization
    - 5.1.1 Site offices & camps
    - 5.1.2 Access road construction / improvement
    - 5.1.3 Temporary works (bridges, culverts)
  5.2 Foundation works
    - 5.2.1 Foundation excavation
    - 5.2.2 Reinforcement and formwork
    - 5.2.3 Concrete pouring & curing
    - 5.2.4 Foundation backfill and protection
  5.3 Tower assembly areas and preparation
  5.4 Drainage, erosion control, and environmental mitigation

6. Tower Fabrication, Assembly & Erection
  6.1 Tower fabrication (if in scope)
    - 6.1.1 Fabrication quality control
    - 6.1.2 Galvanizing and finish
    - 6.1.3 Factory acceptance tests
  6.2 Tower delivery to site
  6.3 Tower assembly & erection
    - 6.3.1 Assembly of tower sections
    - 6.3.2 Erection (crane-assisted or gin-pole methods)
    - 6.3.3 Bolting, torqueing, and NDT as required
  6.4 Tower surveying and alignment

7. Conductor Stringing & Line Works
  7.1 Sag-tension calculations and preparation
  7.2 Pilot line & messenger installation
  7.3 Pulling, tensioning and sagging conductors
    - 7.3.1 Conductor splicing and jointing
    - 7.3.2 Installation of vibration dampers and spacers
  7.4 Shield wire/OPGW installation (if applicable)
  7.5 Grounding & earthing of towers
  7.6 Insulator and hardware installation

8. Substation & Interconnection Works
  8.1 Substation civil works (if included)
  8.2 Installation of transformers, switchgear, and busbars
  8.3 Protection & control system installation
  8.4 Fiber/communication integration
  8.5 Secondary systems testing and configuration

9. Testing, Commissioning & Energization
  9.1 Pre-commissioning checks
    - 9.1.1 Mechanical inspections
    - 9.1.2 Electrical continuity and insulation testing
    - 9.1.3 Protection relay configuration checks
  9.2 System commissioning (no-load tests, functional tests)
  9.3 Trial energization and synchronization
  9.4 Performance verification and acceptance testing
  9.5 Safety verification and sign-off

10. Environment, Social & HSE Management
  10.1 Implementation of environmental management measures
  10.2 Community liaison and grievance mechanisms
  10.3 Health & safety compliance monitoring
  10.4 Emergency response planning

11. Operations Readiness & Handover
  11.1 Training of O&M staff
  11.2 As-built documentation compilation
  11.3 Spare parts and tools handover
  11.4 Handover certificate and acceptance

12. Close-out & Lessons Learned
  12.1 Final documentation and archive
  12.2 Financial close-out
  12.3 Lessons learned workshop

Appendix — Recommended fields for each WBS item
- ID: WBS number
- Name: Short descriptive name
- Owner: Person / role responsible
- Description: Brief summary of work
- Start date / End date
- Duration: Estimated effort
- Cost estimate
- Dependencies: Other WBS IDs
- Deliverables: What will be delivered
- Acceptance criteria
- Risk notes
